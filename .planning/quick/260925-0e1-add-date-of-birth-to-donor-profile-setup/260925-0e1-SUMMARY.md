## Decisions

**Nullable column, not `NOT NULL`.** A `profiles` row is created at OTP verification
with only `id` + `phone` (`App.tsx` `handleVerified`), well before donor setup, and
requesters who never donate never supply a DOB. `NOT NULL` would break every new
signup — and could not have been applied over the 15 existing rows anyway. "Required"
lives in the donor form; the DB enforces the age rule on whatever value arrives.

**`CHECK` against `current_date`.** Normally a dump/restore hazard, but safe in this
direction: people only get older, so a row that satisfies the constraint today
satisfies it forever.

**Three selects over `<input type="date">`.** The native mobile date picker opens on
today, so a donor scrolls back thirty-odd years every time. Dropdowns jump straight to
the year and carry Burmese month names and numerals, which the OS calendar cannot.
